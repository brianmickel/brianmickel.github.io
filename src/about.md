## Colophon

Built with [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/) and deployed to GitHub Pages; the [source](https://github.com/brianmickel/brianmickel.github.io) is public. Set in Google Sans Flex, Google Sans Text and Google Sans Mono. The name on the front page is a variable font whose weight and width follow your cursor, in about seventy lines of JavaScript and no library. The off-white, the ink rules and the 10px unit are borrowed from [Project Zero](https://projectzero.google/).
