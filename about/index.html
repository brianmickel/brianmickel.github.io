About

Software engineer. I build things and write down what I learn along the way.

This site collects what I've built, going back to a euchre tournament scheduler in 2015, and what I learned making it. Everything is dated to when it was originally built, not when I wrote it up. Entries are tagged by topic: Energy & Renewables, Board games, CAS, and Cycling & Infrastructure.

Elsewhere: GitHub, LinkedIn.

Colophon

Built with Eleventy and deployed to GitHub Pages; the source is public. Set in Google Sans Flex, Google Sans Text and Google Sans Mono. The name on the front page is a variable font whose weight and width follow your cursor, in about seventy lines of JavaScript and no library. The off-white, the ink rules and the 10px unit are borrowed from Project Zero.
